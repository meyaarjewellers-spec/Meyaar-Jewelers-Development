# Meyaar Jewellers Design Guidelines

## Design Approach
Reference-based approach inspired by **gorjana.com** - adopting their clean layout, breathable spacing, product display style, and typography cleanliness while adapting to a handcrafted, limited-edition artisan aesthetic.

## Core Design Elements

### Typography
- **Primary Font**: Modern serif for headings and hero text (elegant, refined)
- **Secondary Font**: Clean sans-serif for body text, navigation, and UI elements
- **Hierarchy**: Large, elegant headings with generous letter-spacing; clean readable body text

### Layout System
**Spacing**: Use Tailwind units of **4, 6, 8, 12, 16** for consistent rhythm (p-4, p-6, p-8, m-12, py-16, etc.)
- Generous whitespace throughout
- Breathable padding between sections (py-16 to py-24 on desktop)
- Product grids with consistent gaps (gap-6 to gap-8)

### Component Library

**Homepage**
- Large hero section (80vh) with full-width background image showcasing handmade jewelry
- Centered headline: "Handcrafted Artisan Luxury" with prominent CTA button (blurred background)
- Featured collections grid (3 cards: Necklaces, Bracelets, Earrings) with hover effects
- Brand story highlight section with imagery
- Instagram feed grid (4-6 images)
- Newsletter signup with elegant form
- Customer testimonials/reviews carousel
- Minimal footer with logo, navigation, social links

**Product Category Pages**
- Clean card grid layout (3-4 columns on desktop, 2 on tablet, 1 on mobile)
- Soft shadows on product cards
- Quick-view hover overlay on cards
- Product image, title, price cleanly displayed

**Product Detail Pages**
- Large product image gallery (left side, 60% width)
- Product details panel (right side, 40% width)
- "Handcrafted in limited quantity" badge prominently placed
- Materials, dimensions, care details in expandable sections
- Clear add-to-cart button
- Related items carousel below

**About Page**
- Full-width hero with brand story
- Multi-column layout showcasing craftsmanship process
- Artisan photography integrated throughout
- Timeline or story sections with warm brown accent lines

**Navigation**
- Minimal top header with logo left-aligned
- Clean horizontal menu: Shop (dropdown: Necklaces, Bracelets, Earrings), About, Contact
- Cart icon with item count
- Sticky on scroll

**Mobile Optimization**
- Hamburger menu for navigation
- Single column product grid
- Sticky bottom add-to-cart bar on product pages
- Touch-friendly spacing (minimum 44px tap targets)

### Animations
- Subtle fade-in on scroll for sections
- Smooth hover transitions on product cards (gentle lift, shadow increase)
- Smooth page transitions
- Keep animations minimal and refined

## Images
**Hero Section**: Large, high-quality lifestyle image showcasing jewelry pieces on model or elegant display (full-width, 80vh height)

**Product Cards**: Professional product photography on neutral backgrounds, square aspect ratio

**About Page**: Artisan workshop photos, close-up craftsmanship shots, brand story imagery

**Instagram Feed**: Social media lifestyle images in grid format

All images should maintain the warm, handcrafted luxury aesthetic with soft lighting and neutral backgrounds.
